SCORING DETAILS

A review index, with the math in view.

CityFix combines normalized evidence into a 0–100 index. Each count is scaled against the 95th percentile across all locations, then capped at 100. That keeps unusually large values from dominating the queue.

ML forecast: a 36-tree Random Forest regressor estimates Q4 incident counts from Q1–Q3 incidents and complaints plus street context. Results include a location holdout test and mean-baseline comparison. The review index remains a separate, transparent heuristic. Both use synthetic data and require local validation before operational use.
